# About Me:

I am a seasoned developer with over eight years of industry experience, including three years in tech leadership, with my primary stack being PHP and TypeScript while expanding my expertise in DevOps practices to drive further innovation. My unique strength lies in my diverse experience working with both enterprise applications (implementing Clean Architecture, Domain-Driven Design, and TDD principles) and startups with junior developers where code often needs improvement paths, giving me a deep understanding of priorities and the skills to introduce good practices into teams without causing disruption or resistance. By applying these methodologies, I consistently improved code quality, successfully worked in enterprise environments, and independently delivered a minimum viable product (MVP) for a startup that now serves more than 500 businesses while leading a team of four developers. I consistently apply SOLID principles and focus on modular, reusable architecture that scales with business growth.

# Skills
* Languages & Frameworks: PHP, TypeScript, JavaScript, .NET, Angular, React
* Architecture: Clean Architecture, Domain-Driven Design
* Cloud & DevOps: AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft PowerPlatform
* Databases: PostgreSQL, MySQL
